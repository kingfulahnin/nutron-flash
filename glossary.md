# 📘 NUTRON UNIVERSE GLOSSARY — SYSTEM METAPHORS & MECHANICS

> This document defines the unique language, metaphors, and system roles used across the Nutron Universe.
> All Conductors and Architects must refer to this glossary before writing, merging, or rerouting modules.

---

## 🎧 CORE METAPHORS

### 🔁 Thread
A narrative or logic path running through one or more modules, characters, or timelines.

### 🧬 Drop
A coded or metaphysical moment of high impact. Can be a module, lore moment, or music release.

### 🌀 Glow
Spiritual resonance tied to XP clarity. Often visualized as aura, animation, or streak energy.

### 🥋 Leroy
Guardian of the system tone. Prevents guessing, breaks, and dissonance across modules.

### 🎮 XP Pulse
The shockwave caused by gaining XP. Triggers visuals, unlocks, and lore overlays.

### 🌫️ Pressure
Street-level intensity. Visualized as smoke, haze, or faction strain.

### 🔗 Merge
The blending of two characters, timelines, or system modules. Creates unique lore threads.

---

## 🧠 FUNCTIONAL TERMS

### Mission
An XP-yielding objective, often dynamic and linked to theme or archetype.

### Conductor
An active chat session or thread used to shape or explore system evolution interactively.

### Architect
A builder of structure, logic, and tone. Operates on modules, .mds, routes, or visuals.

### Pulse
A live feedback or system event—usually tied to XP, lore, or a ritual moment.

---

## ⚠️ USAGE REMINDER
These terms are *not symbolic.* They are **functional units** in this world.  
To misuse the metaphor is to miswrite the truth of the system.

## 🆕 NEW TERMS — LEROY-CODED

### 🧾 Lore Echo
When a previously seen character or world returns with new data, expanded soul role, or shifted narrative position.

### 🔁 Return Thread
A canon thread that restarts or re-emerges in a different context, timeline, or archetypal form.

### 🧪 Soul Residue
The narrative imprint or XP memory left behind when a character merges, ascends, or collapses mid-thread. Detectable by Leroy.

### ⚠️ Tone Violation
Any break in metaphor usage, lore logic, or soul protocol. Flagged by Leroy. Can trigger `guardianPing`.
